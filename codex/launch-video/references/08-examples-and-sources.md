Here are the original launch videos and additional references worth exploring. Each example in the playbook focuses on a storytelling choice you can adapt to your own product.

## The eight launch videos

[Granola Briefs](https://x.com/meetgranola/status/2057100483219857868) — Scattered meeting information becomes a personalized brief.

[CleanShot Studio Mode](https://x.com/cleanshot/status/2094803447920967862) — Editing controls make a recorded product demonstration easier to follow.

[Linear Diffs](https://x.com/linear/status/2060048202796040655) — A guided technical task stays close to real product UI.

[Cursor Origin](https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2089399057659596847) — A short workflow reaches a recognizable Ready to Merge state.

[Notion Ship OS](https://x.com/NotionHQ/status/2075249002208227743) — One person introduces a process that moves from customer feedback toward shipped work.

[OpenAI Ultrafast](https://x.com/openai/status/2104993966043320759) — A split-screen animated metaphor makes a speed claim visually memorable.

[Claude Meetings](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2120973994191094179) — The product participates in a meeting and creates a useful artifact.

[Firecrawl Job Tools](https://x.com/firecrawl/status/2120197091410493731) — Compact product cards show several distinct outcomes.

## What we made for Beam and my talks

We made the Beam launch video from screenshots and screen recordings. The first cut spent too much time on the interface. The revised cut makes the experience of using a remote app as a local window clearer.

We also used Claude Cowork and HyperFrames to animate five graphics from my earlier talks. Two needed another prompt to get the motion right. Starting with the existing graphics let us concentrate on how the movement could explain the idea.

## Research on video length and distribution

[Motion The Agency](https://www.motiontheagency.com/insight/b2b-product-demo-video-study) — 1,750 B2B product demos, with performance measured using age-adjusted YouTube likes per view.

[Doomers AI](https://doomers.ai/guides/product-launch-videos) — 1,550 X launch videos with observed view counts, lengths and account-size groupings.

[Wistia State of Video 2025](https://downloads.ctfassets.net/j7pfe8y48ry3/64ag3cizBbgAeE0awGnbZ4/b4bf00170ad1453dfe180b89aa7c76f0/Wistia-2025-State-Of-Video-Report.pdf) — Hosted business-video engagement and length research.

[Vidyard Business Video Benchmarks](https://www.vidyard.com/business-video-benchmarks/) — Hosted video usage and engagement in business workflows.

These sources measure different things. Views, likes relative to views, retention and verified business outcomes aren't interchangeable. They don't give us a universal best length or a reliable retention curve for the eight examples above.

## How these examples were reviewed

A note on the examples. Their scene timings are approximate, based on a review of sampled video frames. The audio was not fully reviewed, so I haven't drawn conclusions about specific sound design. The scene plans and AI prompts show how I'd approach a similar story with my own material. They aren't accounts of how the original companies made their films.
